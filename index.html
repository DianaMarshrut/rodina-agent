Rodina Agent

Rodina Agent is a private internal assistant used by its owner, Anna Rodina, to work with her own Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs files: finding documents, reading and updating spreadsheets, and drafting documents on her instruction.

The app is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. Only the owner's Google account is connected.

Contact: anhen1986@gmail.com
